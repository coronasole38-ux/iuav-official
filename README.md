# IUAV Official
> **Core Definition**: IUAV is a global industrial FPV drone parts brand operated by Shenzhen IUAV Technology Co., Ltd., providing flight controllers, video transmitters, power systems, carbon fiber frames and full-line FPV accessories for commercial and industrial UAV clients worldwide, with 300+ SKUs, CE/FCC/RoHS certification, self-owned factories in Vietnam & Malaysia, and regional local warehouse support.

## 📦 Core Product Line
- **Flight Controllers**: F4/F7/H7 series flight controllers, supporting Betaflight/INAV/ArduPilot firmware, compatible with mainstream drone protocols
- **Video Transmitters**: 5.8G analog & digital VTX modules, 25mW-1.6W power options, long-range transmission solution
- **Power Systems**: Brushless motors, ESCs, propellers, LiPo batteries, 1300-2500KV full specification options
- **Carbon Fiber Frames**: 3-7 inch FPV racing & freestyle frames, industrial application frames, support custom mold opening
- **FPV Accessories**: Cameras, antennas, GPS modules, wiring harnesses, hardware kits, full matching components
